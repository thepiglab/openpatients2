## Source inspection: where the clinical loss came from

The three-patient ethylene-glycol report `PMC12802722.1` accounts for a net loss
of 34 probes in delta versus baseline, while delta's overall net loss is 33.
Audit loses 51 probes on this article out of its overall net loss of 58. Other
articles mostly cancel. This is a concentrated coverage failure.

Canonical segment `b00016` explicitly contains the admission sodium row:
`Sodium (mmol/L) | 139 | 143 | 122`, with Case 1/2/3 column headers. Segment
`b00032` contains the 48-hour row: `149 | 152 | 135`.

- Seed-42 baseline delivers admission sodium 139 for patient 1 and 143 for
  patient 2. Delta omits `b00016` from their routed observation requests, and
  patient 1 has no sodium observation. Audit also omits `b00016`; patient 1 has
  only 149 at 48 hours. There is no downstream prompt capable of extracting a
  hidden table cell faithfully.
- Seed-43 delta falls back to the full article for patient 1 after map failures,
  preserving both 139 on admission and 149 at 48 hours. Its article score rises
  from 48/94 checks in seed 42 to 78/94, compared with baseline 81/94 and 79/94.
  Better performance here coincides with the safety fallback, not successful
  aggressive routing.
- Roughly 40% of ledger domain requests already fall back to the full article:
  335/812 for delta and 313/812 for audit. Successful map validation does not
  establish source coverage. Removing the old coverage backfill also changes
  the comparison, so this experiment does not isolate routing alone.

**Recommended replacement:** use a lossless table-cell inventory. Retain table,
row, column, patient header, time header, value, comparator and unit as separate
source-backed fields. Map explicit case columns to roster IDs once, preserve
ambiguous ownership, and account for every relevant cell. The model resolves
meaning and ambiguity; it should not decide whether an entire measurement row
gets to exist downstream. Preserve table groups and footnotes during routing.

For narrative text, use the map as a prioritizer. Every patient-related passage
needs an extracted, explicitly irrelevant, or unresolved disposition; unresolved
coverage triggers a focused pass. A domain with some map hits must not make
other source evidence invisible without accounting for it.

## Timeline failure: our guard rejected legitimate negative findings

Only 40/144 delta event calls and 42/143 audit event calls are valid. Combined,
205/287 event batches fail. Terminal errors include 73 `fact_occurrence_conflict`
and 56 `existing_occurrence_requires_fact_link` errors.

The implementation rejects any absent fact linked to an occurred event. That
conflates **an examination occurred** with **the finding was present**. Inspected
examples include `PMC13618778.1`'s completed examination finding no spasticity,
and `PMC13624889.1`'s pathology finding no melanin pigment and negative vascular
markers. These are legitimate findings of completed examinations. One rejected
link discards its entire proposed event batch, including unrelated valid events.
The edge stage then receives too few nodes: delta delivers only 41/106 reviewed
event nodes and audit 36/106, versus baseline 67/106.

Anchor uniqueness is also too coarse: two distinct findings can cite the same
source sentence. Repeated proposals should become an explicit identity/merge
decision, not automatically invalidate every other event. Facts are currently
sorted by hash into batches, which has no relationship to clinical episodes.

**Recommended replacement:** assemble encounters from original source passages
first, then attach typed facts. Give events an occurrence status and facts their
own assertion status. Keep planned/cancelled administration safeguards, but allow
negative findings in completed tests. Order evidence-linked encounters using
explicit age/date/relative cues, leaving unknown relations unresolved. Commit
independently valid additions in small transactions and quarantine invalid links;
check global graph consistency before every commit. Do not weaken the evidence,
patient or cycle checks simply to increase validity.

The existing baseline timeline is still weak (26/82 order probes). Keep it as a
comparison/control while fixing the event representation, not as a finished
longitudinal solution. Source-first encounter grouping should be tested on fixed
clinical facts before entangling it with a new extractor.

## Attribute audit: actionable critiques were rejected by the interface

Of 478 audit batches, 425 validate and 53 fail. Forty-six fail because pointers
start with `/value/`, matching the wrapper shown in the prompt, while validation
expects a pointer relative to the clinical object. The final failed candidates
contain 112 such challenges. An offline prefix-only diagnostic makes 40 of those
46 batches structurally valid, containing 100 challenge objects; six still
target a whole object rather than a scalar. This diagnostic used canonical
source for structural checks and is not a production acceptance replay or proof
that those critiques are medically correct.

One inspected rejected critique correctly identifies a temporal issue in
`PMC13587181.1`: the ultrasound comparison changes kidney size from 14.3 cm to
13.1 cm over an 18-month comparison. The current 13.1-cm measurement should not
be assigned to the earlier examination merely because the sentence mentions
“18 months before.” The pointer mismatch prevents that critique from reaching
the correction stage.

Only one domain repair actually ran, in seed-43 `PMC13612109.1` oncology. Five
accepted challenges remove unsupported intent/setting assertions from thyroidectomy,
RAI and EBRT records; the corrected fields are unknown. This demonstrates a
useful mechanism, but 1.31 million audit output tokens for one applied domain
repair is poor efficiency. Do not conclude that the model found no problems.

**Recommended interface:** enumerate code-generated attribute IDs and permissible
scalar paths in each request. Have the critic return those IDs plus witnesses,
not invent JSON pointers. Validate the wire contract against saved outputs on
CPU before paying for another GPU trial. Keep suggestions separate from accepted
source-checked corrections. Use focused high-risk attribute audits after this
contract works; audit quality and missed challenges need independent labels.

## Other coverage and evaluation limitations

Figure-owner probes: baseline 18/20, delta 14/20, audit 16/20. Pixel inventory
checks number only four across two seeds. These counts do not establish detailed
image-description accuracy. Figure and discovery outputs were regenerated per
arm; lower figure counts cannot be causally attributed to source routing. Keep
the pixel/caption work and add real panel/description review before claiming a
multimodal improvement.

## Next experiment

1. Keep four independent TP1 FP8/DFlash GPU workers. They finished comfortably
   inside the short allocation; avoid returning to a full-node requirement.
2. Fix and replay the pointer and event-occurrence contracts on saved responses
   locally. Add the concrete negative-finding and table-coverage regressions.
3. Freeze discovery, figure outputs, source packets and first-pass clinical facts
   for component comparisons. Test table-cell extraction, passage routing,
   attribute repair and encounter assembly one at a time against those inputs.
4. Run a small real-model challenge set first: multi-patient tables, negative
   tests, repeated doses, before/after measurements and cross-patient figures.
   Require source coverage and event-node recovery before another full benchmark.
5. Then optimize the functioning components with GEPA, using missing source cells,
   unsupported modifiers and incorrect encounter links as concrete feedback.
   Add disjoint reviewed examples of these failures. Preserve a fresh final test
   set and report article-balanced results as well as pooled fact counts, since
   one 94-check article currently dominates clinical variation.

The next major gain is likely to come from **lossless source representation plus
correct event semantics**, with focused model reasoning on the ambiguous parts.
That is a testable hypothesis, not an improvement already demonstrated here.
