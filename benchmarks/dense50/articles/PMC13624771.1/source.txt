Successful gastric endoscopic submucosal dissection using an external second bending device

[b00001] Article
Endoscopic submucosal dissection (ESD) of the greater curvature of the gastric body is technically challenging due to its limited stability., In the forward view, the endoscope often floats within the lumen, rendering it susceptible to displacement by respiratory movements. Retroflexion may be attempted; however, considerable forward pressure is required in the lower gastric body, resulting in paradoxical scope movements and impaired maneuverability.

[b00002] Article
The AttachBend (Fujifilm) is a novel external device that creates a second bending point at the distal end of a thin therapeutic endoscope (EG-840TP; Fujifilm) (Fig. 1). The angle of the second bend could be easily adjusted using the handle at the proximal end. We report a successful ESD utilizing this device for management of lesions in the greater curvature of the lower gastric body (Video 1).

[b00003] Article
An 82-year-old woman presented with a 20-mm depressed lesion (Fig. 2). ESD was performed using an EG-840TP with the device set to down-angulation direction. For insertion, the device was covered with a transparent film that to smooth the surface, and even after multiple reinsertions, no pharyngeal or esophageal injuries were observed. Combining the down-angulation of the second bend with the up-angulation of the scope created an S-shaped configuration that maintained several centimeters of the shaft in continuous mucosal contact (Fig. 3). This setup significantly enhanced the scope stability and minimized the effects of respiratory movements. A mucosal flap was created on the oral side, followed by a circumferential incision. Submucosal dissection was performed in the forward view. The lesion was successfully resected en bloc without adverse events.

[b00004] Article
This external device improved the maneuverability in anatomically unfavorable locations, enabling safer and more efficient treatments. Although a comparison with underwater ESD or traction devices requires further study, the additional cost may be justified in selected difficult cases where stable ESD cannot otherwise be achieved.

AttachBend (Fujifilm) is an external attachment device designed to provide an additional bending point to the distal portion of a therapeutic endoscope, with a maximum angulation of 70°.
