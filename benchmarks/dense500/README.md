# Dense 500-article annotation project

Target: 500 purposefully varied PMC articles. Work is in progress: downloaded,
annotated, source-reviewed, and validated counts are distinct. A selected article
is not a completed annotation. Do not train or score against unreviewed drafts.

GPT-6.1 Sol annotators read original article text independently of prior model
predictions. The parent assistant subsequently reviews each final annotation
against its source. These are AI reference annotations, not physician-adjudicated
clinical gold. Source inconsistencies and unsupported interpretations remain
explicit; field/quote validation alone does not establish medical correctness.

The primary tracker is `../../data/dense_clinical_cases_v1/article_checklist.json`,
regenerated by `export.py`: one entry keyed by article ID with a `completed`
boolean, individual review checks, and remaining work. Never infer a review
from notes or from a successful validator run; only signed receipts count.

The first 23 retained articles are in `../dense50/` while their initial annotation
batch finishes. New acquisitions are stored here. Sources retain individual
rights decisions and attribution. Eligible text/XML and small, article-specific
figure assets are fetched with bounded storage. Selected PDF supplements are
downloaded and their actual pages inspected when needed; no model checkpoints
or bulk media collection is downloaded. Supplement asset exceptions remain
separate from article licensing.

Selection uses 24 clinical/species strata and explicit, reproducible eligibility
rules. It is a challenge set, not a random sample of PMC or an estimate of disease
prevalence. Previously benchmarked articles are restricted to training. New
source families will be assigned to train/validation/test before annotation.

Figure captions and patient/panel ownership are annotated. Pixel interpretation
must stay `not_inspected` unless an annotator actually views the image; captions
must not be presented as visual inspection. Unavailable tables/supplements are
recorded as limitations rather than silently treated as negative evidence.
