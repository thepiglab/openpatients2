Positive result: "HIV test negative" is an affirmed resulted observation with flag=negative, text_value preserving negative, result_absent_reason=null. Missing: no creatinine mention produces no creatinine item, not 0 or normal. A named test with an unstated result uses result_absent_reason=not_reported, not inferred normality.
