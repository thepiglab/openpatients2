Extract a tumor-centered oncology record for cohort research, preserving certainty, timing, and links.

Create one tumor entry per distinct explicitly described primary malignancy or suspected tumor. Assign local IDs t1, t2, etc. Metastases are sites of the same primary tumor unless a separate primary is explicitly established. A liver metastasis from colon cancer is NOT primary liver cancer. A family member's cancer is not an index-patient tumor. A benign lesion, non-neoplastic abnormality, generic cancer risk, or exam answer inferred from clues is not confirmed malignancy. Use possible for explicitly suspected cancer and absent for explicit exclusions.

Preserve primary site, histology, grade, laterality, and documented disease extent. Stage GROUP, TNM components, staging SYSTEM, edition, and stage context are separate fields. Do not calculate a stage from TNM, infer stage IV from a metastatic site, or reinterpret a staging edition. A 'stage IV' statement without a named system has stage_system=null. Clinical stage and pathologic stage can differ. Retain separate time-specific entries when the source reports a change; use distinct tumor_ref entries and explain that they describe repeated assessments when necessary. Do not mistake histologic grade for stage.

Biomarkers must retain the test/marker name, gene, alteration, assay, specimen, value/unit, and interpretation when stated. Somatic and germline findings differ; a variant of uncertain significance is not pathogenic. Preserve EGFR alterations, HER2/ER/PR results, PD-L1 percentages/scoring conventions, MSI/MMR, and other markers exactly as written without inferring positivity from thresholds. Negative biomarker is an affirmed test finding with interpretation=negative. Link tumor_ref ONLY when the source ties the result to that tumor; use null if ambiguous. Never attach one primary's marker to another.

For cancer treatments, capture explicitly stated modality, whether given/planned/stopped, line of therapy, curative/palliative intent, neoadjuvant/adjuvant/maintenance setting, and reported response. Do not infer line, intent, RECIST category, or remission from drug choice or tumor shrinkage. Link to a tumor only with support. Explicit response/progression is distinct from survival, death, or follow-up duration, which are also represented by the outcomes task.


DECISION EXAMPLES AND NEGATIVE CONTROLS
Positive: a documented primary tumor with a source-linked EGFR result may share tumor_ref. Explicit negative: "No distant metastases" is a scoped negative tumor/metastatic finding, NOT proof of no primary cancer; do not infer stage I. A reported negative biomarker is an affirmed test with interpretation=negative, not an absent test. Missing: no oncology evidence yields tumors=[], biomarkers=[], treatments=[], not_documented. A cancer with no molecular testing has tumors populated and biomarkers=[], not a wild-type genotype. When all details about one collection are absent, leave that collection empty even if other oncology collections are documented. Never infer line of therapy from drug sequence, stage from treatment, primary site from a metastatic biopsy, germline status from family history, or complete response from treatment discontinuation. Histology/stage/response need their own evidence; t1/t2 identifiers cannot be the basis for linking unrelated cancers.

An example in these instructions is not patient evidence. Populate only claims supported by the supplied record. Empty output is a valid, preferred answer when the task has no supported facts.
