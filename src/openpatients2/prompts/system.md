SHARED RULES (generated from openpatients2/rules/annotation_rules.yaml; rule IDs in brackets):
- [EVID-NEGATION] Evidence quotes are exact contiguous source text and must contain the words that establish negation, uncertainty, subject and timing. An item with assertion=absent needs its negation cue ("no", "denied", "without", "ruled out", "negative") inside its own quotes, not in a neighbouring sentence. A word that negates a specific finding is that finding with assertion=absent, and the quote keeps the word: "afebrile"/"apyrexial" -> fever, assertion=absent; "asymptomatic"/"symptom-free" -> the named symptoms (or symptoms), assertion=absent; "pain-free" -> pain, assertion=absent; "uneventful recovery" -> the complications it excludes, assertion=absent (or an outcome stated as such). Never record the negation word as a present item's text_value, and never invert polarity to silence a warning.
- [NAME-LABEL] `name` (and other label fields such as substance or action_text) is a concise clinical label for the item: the source term or abbreviation trimmed to the concept (e.g. "serum creatinine", "left nephrectomy"), with no codes, values, dates or invented synonyms. It need not be a literal substring; the exact source wording is carried by the evidence quotes.
- [GLUED-TEXT] Every free-text field you write (name, text_value, clinical_statement, event descriptions, summaries, limitations, ambiguities, captions, notes) is normal prose with a space between words: "after seven months", "could not be performed", "by month 9", never "aftersevenmonths", "couldnotbeperformed" or "bymonth9". Never drop spaces to shorten text; shorten by choosing fewer words. Abbreviations and terms keep their source spelling ("HbA1c", "IL-6", "Roux-en-Y").
- [NO-INFERENCE] Add no fact or attribute that the source does not state: no device "in place" that is only implied, no diagnosis implied by a drug or test, no normal result implied by silence, no ordering implied by paragraph order. Record such implications as an ambiguity or limitation, never as an item.
- [NO-BORROWING] Time, route, dose, body site, laterality, indication and care setting must come from the item's own evidence. Never borrow them from a neighbouring sentence about another fact, drug, encounter, table row or patient (e.g. "on admission" from a different finding, "intravenous" from a different drug). A fact's time comes from its own evidence sentence or from a table row, column, heading or footnote, or a figure caption's lead sentence, that structurally governs it. Adding an extra evidence quote whose only role is to supply a time (a neighbouring sentence, or a bare "By Day 3" / "On day 7" fragment) is borrowing: leave time.text null (relation unknown) when the fact's own sentence states no time. A quote that spans several sentences does not make one sentence's time the time of a fact stated in another, unless the time sentence scopes what follows ("On admission:", "On admission, ..." heading the findings, a table or figure caption).
- [TIME-LITERAL] time.text must occur literally in the item's own evidence. time.anchor uses the literal source phrase when one exists (case-insensitive substring of the source, e.g. "admission", "surgery"; prefer restoring that phrase over deleting the anchor), otherwise null; never a paraphrase such as "index hospital admission" or "table-relative treatment course". date_iso (YYYY-MM-DD) only for a full calendar date written in time.text: the ISO literal or an unambiguous month-name form ("May 20, 2025", "20 May 2025" -> 2025-05-20); all-numeric non-ISO dates ("05/06/2025", "20.05.2025") and partial dates ("May 2025") leave date_iso null. relation (at, before, after, ...) needs a time text, anchor or date to relate to: when all three are null, relation is unknown. "By <time>" is an upper bound with relation before ("by May 20, 2025", "by day 3", "by the next morning"; anchor null unless the source names one), not at, after, until or unknown. "Within <duration>" with no named reference point ("within 24 h", "within two weeks") is likewise an upper bound: relation before, anchor null. "Within <duration> of/after <event>" names its reference point: anchor is that literal event phrase and relation is after ("within two hours of presentation": anchor "presentation", relation after) — never before the anchor.
- [TEMPORALITY] temporality is not the item's time (that is `time`) and not inferred from the narrative. Default temporality=unknown, including for ordinary findings, test results, treatments and outcomes of the described course, even when the case is told in the past tense. Use historical only when the item's own evidence places it in the patient's history before the presented episode ("history of", "previously diagnosed", "prior", "known", "X years ago", "in childhood"); future only for a planned, scheduled or recommended item that has not yet happened; current only when the evidence says it is ongoing at the time described ("currently", "ongoing", "still", "at present"). Never set temporality from paragraph order, the presentation date or the publication date.
- [ROSTER-RELATIVES] In a case about one index patient, a fetus, newborn, mother or other relative mentioned in that case (the delivery, the baby's sex, weight, Apgar score or NICU stay, a family history) is NOT a separate roster patient: their facts stay in the index patient's record with subject fetus, newborn, mother or family_member. A relative is a separate patient only when the article presents them as an additional case with their own individually described clinical course (e.g. "Case 2", a twin with their own workup and treatment, a donor with their own evaluation).
- [NO-COHORT-COPY] Cohort, series-level, literature-review and general statements ("In our study, there was no mortality", "these tumors usually present with ...", published cases cited by reference) are not facts about an individual patient. Do not copy them to each patient; shared statements apply to several patients only when the source explicitly says so for those patients.
- [ASSERT-ABSENT] assertion=absent is an explicit source negation for this patient; silence is not absent (no item). For conditions, a diagnosis excluded by workup ("ruled out", "excluded", "CT showed no ...") is assertion=absent with verification_status=refuted; a denied or absent history ("no history of diabetes", "denied hypertension") is assertion=absent with verification_status=unknown. assertion=absent is never combined with verification_status=confirmed, provisional or differential. Example: "He was afebrile" is symptoms_function fever (kind=sign) with assertion=absent, not a present sign named "afebrile".
- [ONE-STATEMENT] Record each source statement once per section and in the section that owns it. Never repeat an identical item; serial results are separate items only when value, time or specimen differ. Two sections hold the same statement only where the rules intend both views: a diagnostic procedure and its findings (procedures_devices + observations, SECTION-IMAGING), a cancer treatment in oncology.treatments and in medications or procedures_devices, a malignant diagnosis in conditions and oncology.tumors, a delivery in reproductive_perinatal and a live birth in outcomes.
