Capture mobility, activities of daily living, dependence, oxygen-related activity limits, cognition, consciousness, and stated ECOG, Karnofsky, Glasgow Coma Scale, NIHSS, or other scores when written. Do not calculate scores from narrative findings. A reported ECOG score is a performance_score item in this task, not a disease or an observations item. Preserve the exact scale name, value, and assessment context; a baseline score and a later score should be separate items.

Do not convert a symptom constellation into a syndrome or definitive disease. Do not transfer the mother's symptoms to a newborn or a relative's disability to the index patient. Symptoms of a hypothetical differential or a discussion paragraph are not experienced symptoms. Historical deficits, recovered symptoms, and current deficits must remain temporally distinct.

Use numeric_value only when the value is explicitly written, text_value for qualitative descriptions, and unit when stated. Quantitative vitals and laboratory tests belong in observations, not duplicated here merely as signs. Include negative/uncertain assertion and evidence showing that qualifier. Missing symptom reporting does not establish normal function.
